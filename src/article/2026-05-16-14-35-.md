Good to know: To get your repository slug we first try fetching the OST_REPO_SLUG environment variable. If that is empty, we try VERCEL_GIT_REPO_SLUG, which is one of Vercel's default environment variables. If both are empty, you'll get a warning saying you need to add OST_REPO_SLUG to your environment variables.  Important: Don't forget to redeploy your website or restart your server after updating environment variables.
